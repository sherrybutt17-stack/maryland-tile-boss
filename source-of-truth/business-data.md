## FAQ currently on site (verbatim)

**1. Who is the Maryland Tile Boss?**

The Maryland Tile Boss is a tile industry resource focused on sharing practical knowledge, installation techniques, product information, industry trends and lessons learned from decades of hands-on experience. Our goal is to help contractors, installers, remodelers, builders, DIYers and homeowners make better decisions about tile and tile installation.

**2. What is the Maryland Tile Boss Blog?**

The Maryland Tile Boss Blog is our ongoing source for tile tips, installation techniques, product information, waterproofing, tools, materials, design trends and industry education. We cover both professional-level topics and practical information for homeowners and DIYers.

**3. Who is the Tile Boss website for?**

Everyone interested in tile! Our content is designed for professional tile installers, contractors, remodelers, builders, designers, homeowners and DIYers who want to learn more about tile and the proper way to install it.

**4. Does the Tile Boss provide installation advice?**

Yes. We share general information about tile installation, surface preparation, waterproofing, setting materials, layout, grouting, tools and installation techniques. The goal is to help you understand the process and make more informed decisions.

**5. What should I know before starting a tile project?**

Proper preparation is one of the most important parts of a successful tile installation. The substrate, waterproofing, tile selection, layout, setting materials, movement joints and installation method all need to be considered before the first tile is installed.

**6. Why is shower waterproofing so important?**

Tile and grout are not the same thing as a waterproofing system. A properly designed and installed waterproofing system helps protect the structure behind the tile from water intrusion. The Tile Boss regularly discusses different waterproofing methods and shower systems so you can better understand your options.

**7. What is the difference between waterproofing systems?**

There are several types of shower waterproofing systems, including sheet membranes, liquid-applied membranes and preformed shower systems. Each system has its own installation requirements, advantages and considerations. Always follow the manufacturer's installation instructions for the specific system being used.
