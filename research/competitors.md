# Competitor & SERP Intelligence — Tile Market, Edgewater / Annapolis / Anne Arundel County, MD

**Client:** Digitile International (legacy) / The Maryland Tile Boss (current) — 14 Mayo Rd, Edgewater, MD 21037
**Research date:** 2026-09-03 / 2026-09-04
**Method:** Live web searches + direct HTTP fetches of competitor HTML (title/meta/H1/H2, JSON-LD `@type` extraction, word counts, sitemap enumeration). Every claim below traces to a search result or a fetched page cited inline.

> **On numbers:** No keyword volume, difficulty, or traffic estimates appear in this document. No such tool was available in this research pass. Where a review count or rating is stated, it came from a page that displayed it, and the source is cited. Ranking order reflects the order results were returned by the search engine used, which approximates but does not guarantee Google's live desktop ordering. Treat ordering as directional, not as a rank-tracker readout.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Three findings dominate everything else:

1. **The client's primary website is dead.** `digitilestore.com` now serves a 114-byte domain-parking stub that JS-redirects to `/lander`. `digitileinternationalshowroom.com` refuses TCP connections entirely. Google still indexes and returns the old `digitilestore.com` URLs in live SERPs — meaning the client is currently ranking on pages that resolve to a parked-domain lander for real searchers. This is an active, revenue-losing emergency, not a to-do item.
2. **Nobody in this market owns the "tile showroom + tile installer" combined position.** The showrooms (Chesapeake Tile & Marble, CST Studio, In Home Stone) have material pages but **zero city/location pages**. The installers (USA Pro Floors, KT Tiles, Brothers Flooring) have city pages but **no showroom, no product inventory**. The client legitimately does both under one roof. No competitor site expresses that combination.
3. **One competitor is far ahead of everyone else technically, and it is not a tile company.** USA Pro Floors is a *flooring* contractor in Severn running a ~95-page programmatic city×service SEO operation with the richest schema stack in the market. It ranks in nearly every installation SERP checked. Everyone else is technically weak.

## 3. Overall SERP shape

**Directory dominance by query type:**

| Query family | Directory pressure | #1 result |
|---|---|---|
| Showroom / store queries | Moderate–High (Yelp, landoftile, CMac) | Manufacturer locator or Yelp |
| "near me" + zip | **Severe** (Yelp ×4) | Manufacturer locator |
| Installation service queries | **Low–Moderate** | Real business service page |
| Backsplash | **Severe** (Angi + programmatic) | Angi |
| Bathroom/shower tile | High (Houzz, Yelp) | Houzz |
| County-level | High + programmatic | Yelp |
| Material queries (porcelain/stone) | Low directory, **high programmatic spam** | Programmatic shell |

**Directories seen:** Yelp (most frequent by far), Angi, Houzz, HomeAdvisor, Porch, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, YellowPages, Procore, Growcycle, homeyou, landoftile.com, CMac.ws, localpages.com, Alignable, chamberofcommerce.com.

**National chains:** Home Depot has an Annapolis store (55 Forest Plaza, 21401) but does **not** rank for the tile queries checked. Floor & Decor has **no Annapolis location** — nearest are Glen Burnie, Baltimore, Capitol Heights, Silver Spring — and did not rank organically. The Tile Shop has Maryland locations but none surfaced in Annapolis-area results. **Conclusion: national chains are not an organic threat in this market. Directories and national programmatic lead-gen sites are.** ([source](https://www.homedepot.com/l/S-Annapolis/MD/Annapolis/21401/2589), [source](https://www.yellowpages.com/annapolis-md/floor-and-decor))

**Local pack:** The search tooling available did not expose Google's Local Map Pack directly, so pack composition could not be verified. Businesses repeatedly named across local-intent results — the likely pack candidates — are: **Compass Stone & Tile Studio / CST Studio, In Home Stone, Chesapeake Tile & Marble, Annapolis Carpet Flooring America, Modern Carpet One, South River Flooring, Annapolis Tile Center, KT Tiles, USA Pro Floors**. This should be verified manually from an Edgewater-geolocated browser before any pack strategy is finalized.

## 7. Top 10 gap opportunities, ranked

**0. (Prerequisite, not optional) Fix the dead-domain emergency.**
`digitilestore.com` serves a 200-OK parking lander on URLs Google still ranks. Either recover the domain and 301 every legacy URL to the new site, or — if it cannot be recovered — treat reclaiming that indexed footprint as the launch's first job. `digitileinternationalshowroom.com` (ECONNREFUSED) needs the same decision. **No SEO work matters until traffic stops landing on a parking page.** Also consolidate the two duplicate Yelp listings and settle on one brand name and one phone number before building citations.

**1. Own "Edgewater" completely — zero competitors contest it.**
Not one competitor has an Edgewater-specific tile page. The current top three for `tile store Edgewater MD` are manufacturer/franchise locator pages (American Olean, Carpet One), and the client already holds three of ten slots there with dead URLs. A real Edgewater showroom page plus Edgewater service pages should take the top of that SERP quickly.

**2. Build the material × city page type that literally nobody has.**
`porcelain tile Annapolis MD` returns **three national programmatic shells in the top three** (canyontileservice.com, strivelaminatefloorinstallation.com, getnaturalstonepolishing.com). CST Studio's `/porcelain` is the only real local material page and it has no geography. Genuinely local material pages — porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, glass, mosaic, large-format — each crossed with Annapolis / Edgewater / Anne Arundel, is a page type with no incumbent.

**3. Take the backsplash SERP — the weakest commercial SERP in the market.**
`kitchen backsplash installer Annapolis MD` is Angi at #1 followed by call-center subdomains (855/877 numbers). Exactly one real local business ranks. A dedicated backsplash page with real project photos, material options, pattern guidance (herringbone/chevron/subway/Zellige) and local pricing wins this outright.

**4. Own shower and wet-area tile on technical authority.**
No true tile specialist has a dedicated shower-tile page; an HVAC company currently outranks most tile firms for `shower tile installation Annapolis`. KT Tiles has the right idea with `/schluter-kerdi` and `/wedi` pages but only 336 words site-wide and `WebSite` schema — it cannot rank them. **The client is Wedi Pro Certified AND Schluter-trained** — a dual credential no competitor claims. Build proper Schluter-Kerdi, Wedi, curbless-shower, steam-shower, and waterproofing-failure pages and take the category on genuine expertise.

**5. Deploy the schema stack seven of eight competitors don't have.**
Market-wide, `FAQPage`, `AggregateRating`, `Service`, `HowTo` and `Product` are essentially absent — only USA Pro Floors uses them, and **`Product` schema is used by literally nobody, including a 41-product WooCommerce store**. A full stack — `LocalBusiness`/`HomeAndConstructionBusiness` + `Service` + `FAQPage` + `Product` + `BreadcrumbList` + `ImageObject` + `OpeningHoursSpecification` — is a structural advantage available for the taking. (Hold `AggregateRating` until the review base is real.)

**6. Publish pricing. Nobody does.**
Not one competitor publishes tile or installation pricing — USA Pro Floors ranks for cost intent while giving no figures, and its `/flooring-cost-calculator/` is its most differentiated asset. Cost-per-square-foot ranges by material, a backsplash cost guide, a shower-tile cost guide, and an interactive estimator would capture an entire intent class the market has abandoned.

**7. Be the only site that answers questions.**
Only USA Pro Floors has FAQ content with schema. Unanswered across the market: porcelain vs ceramic for Chesapeake humidity; how salt air affects grout and sealer; how often to reseal natural stone near the water; large-format tile substrate requirements; why showers fail and what waterproofing prevents it; radiant heat under tile; lead times; how much overage to order; what to bring to a showroom visit. Each is a genuine query with no local answer.

**8. Weaponize the founder story — the strongest E-E-A-T asset in the market.**
Only USA Pro Floors names its owner. **Third-generation installer, Wedi Pro Certified, Schluter-trained, 15+ consecutive years at Coverings, since 2000** — this beats every competitor's credibility copy, and it is currently invisible because it lives only on a dead domain and a JS-rendered UENI page. Surface it site-wide with `Person` schema and author attribution.

**9. Claim the county tier and the showroom page type.**
For `tile contractor Anne Arundel County MD`, USA Pro Floors is the only genuine local business with a county page; the rest of the SERP is programmatic repair-lead spam. Separately, `tile showroom Annapolis MD` is led by Yelp and `landoftile.com` because **no competitor has a real showroom page** — In Home Stone's `/showrooms/` is the only attempt and it ranks below directories. Build a county hub plus a true showroom experience page (25+ vendors, virtual tour, hours, parking, what to expect, free design consultation, book-a-visit CTA).

**10. Serve the trade — a segment with zero competition.**
The client offers **24/7 access for designers, builders and contractors** and free design consultations. No competitor site markets a trade program. A dedicated trade/pro section (designer resources, contractor accounts, spec sheets, samples, lead times) targets high-LTV repeat buyers that nobody is competing for.
