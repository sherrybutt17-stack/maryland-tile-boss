How to choose glass tile for a shower enclosure

Size, finish, colour and slip resistance — how to pick glass tile for a shower without ending up with a surface that is hard to keep clean.

Shower tile and waterproofing Visit the showroom

14 Mayo Rd, Edgewater — Monday to Saturday, 10am–4pm. Closed Sunday.

May 4, 2023 — written by Michael DiGirolamo

If you're looking to add a touch of elegance and sophistication to your bathroom, glass tiles can be an excellent choice for your shower enclosure. Glass tiles are known for their unique appearance, durability, and resistance to water and stains. But with so many options available on the market, choosing the perfect glass tiles for your shower enclosure can be overwhelming. In this blog post, we'll guide you through the essential factors to consider when selecting glass tiles for your shower enclosure, so you can make an informed decision that will result in the perfect shower enclosure.

Consider the size of your shower enclosure.

When selecting glass tiles for your shower enclosure, it's important to consider the size of the area carefully. The size of the tiles can significantly impact the overall look and feel of your shower enclosure. In a small shower enclosure, using larger tiles can make the space feel even smaller, while small tiles can create the illusion of spaciousness.

Small glass tiles are an excellent choice for smaller shower enclosures, as they can create a beautiful, intricate pattern that adds interest to your shower area. Mosaic tiles, for example, are available in small sizes and can be arranged in various patterns, including herringbone or basketweave. This type of pattern can add visual depth to your shower enclosure and create an illusion of space.

On the other hand, if you have a larger shower enclosure, you can use larger glass tiles to create a cohesive look. Large glass tiles can make your shower enclosure look modern and streamlined, with fewer grout lines creating a cleaner and more sophisticated appearance. Larger tiles also make cleaning and maintenance easier since there are fewer grout lines to scrub.

However, it's important to note that larger tiles require more precision when installed. They can be challenging to cut and may need a specialized tile saw. It's best to hire a professional tile installer to ensure that the tiles are cut to the correct size and laid correctly.

Choose a style that fits your bathroom decor.

When choosing glass tiles for your shower enclosure, it's important to keep the overall style of your bathroom in mind. Your shower enclosure should blend seamlessly with the rest of your bathroom to create a cohesive and stylish look. Here are some tips to help you choose the right glass tiles that complement your bathroom decor:

Consider Your Bathroom's Color Scheme: Your bathroom's color scheme is an essential factor to consider when selecting glass tiles. If your bathroom has a neutral color scheme, such as whites, grays, or beige, consider using neutral-colored glass tiles to create a cohesive and minimalist look. Alternatively, if your bathroom has a more vibrant color scheme, such as blues, greens, or yellows, consider using bold-colored glass tiles to add an exciting pop of color.

Evaluate Your Bathroom's Style: The style of your bathroom can also impact the type of glass tiles you choose. If your bathroom has a modern or contemporary style, consider using clean lines and neutral colors for your glass tiles. Glass tiles with a glossy or matte finish can create a sleek and sophisticated look. Alternatively, if your bathroom has a more traditional or vintage style, consider using patterned or textured glass tiles. These tiles can add depth and character to your shower enclosure.

Think About the Mood You Want to Create: Glass tiles can create different moods and atmospheres in your bathroom. If you want to create a relaxing and calming atmosphere, consider using light-colored glass tiles. If you want to add energy and excitement to your bathroom, consider using brightly colored or iridescent glass tiles.

Choose a finish that suits your needs.

The finish of your glass tiles can significantly impact the overall look and feel of your shower enclosure. Each type of finish has its own unique appearance and benefits. Here are some things to consider when choosing the perfect finish for your glass tiles:

Matte Finish: If you prefer a subtle and understated look, a matte finish can be an excellent choice for your glass tiles. Matte finish glass tiles have a flat, non-reflective surface that can create a more natural and organic look. They're also less prone to showing fingerprints or water spots, making them easier to maintain.

Glossy Finish: A glossy finish is ideal if you want to create a sleek and modern look for your shower enclosure. The reflective surface of glossy glass tiles can make your shower enclosure look bigger and brighter. They're also easier to clean since the smooth surface doesn't trap dirt or grime. However, glossy glass tiles are more prone to showing fingerprints and water spots, so they may require more frequent cleaning.

Iridescent Finish: If you want to add a touch of glamor to your bathroom, consider using iridescent glass tiles. Iridescent glass tiles have a unique surface that reflects different colors in different lighting conditions, creating a beautiful and dynamic look. They can also make your shower enclosure look more spacious by reflecting light around the room. However, iridescent glass tiles can be more expensive than other types of glass tiles.

Evaluate the Durability and Maintenance Requirements

Durability and maintenance requirements are essential factors to consider when choosing glass tiles. Glass tiles are durable and resistant to water, stains, and chemicals, making them an excellent choice for your shower enclosure. However, you'll want to ensure that the tiles you choose are easy to clean and maintain. Choose tiles that are resistant to scratches and chips and easy to wipe clean.

Consider the Cost

Finally, it's important to consider the cost when selecting glass tiles for your shower enclosure. Glass tiles can be more expensive than other types of tiles, but their durability and unique appearance can make them a worthwhile investment. Determine your budget and consider the cost of installation when making your final decision.

In conclusion, choosing the perfect glass tiles for your shower enclosure requires careful consideration of several factors, including the size of your shower enclosure, your bathroom decor, the finish, durability, maintenance requirements, and cost. With these factors in mind, you'll be able to choose glass tiles that complement your bathroom design and meet your practical needs.
